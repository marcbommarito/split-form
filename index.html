• Student Transfer Approval

    Menifee Union School District inter-district transfer approval form based on the 2026/2027

  • Before submitting your request, please review the information below.

    Intra-District Transfer (Within Menifee Union School District) An intra-district transfer is a request for your child to attend a different Menifee Union School District school other than their assigned school of residence.

    Inter-District Transfer (Between School Districts) An inter-district transfer is a request for your child to attend a school in a district other than the one where they reside.

    Important Information

    • Please complete all required questions and upload any requested documentation.
    • Transfer requests are reviewed based on District policies, enrollment capacity, program availability, and other applicable criteria.
    • Approval is not guaranteed and may be revoked if the conditions of the transfer are no longer met.
    • Students requesting an intra-district transfer must be enrolled in their assigned Menifee Union School District school before the transfer request can be considered.
    • Families approved for a transfer are responsible for providing transportation to and from school.
    • Approved transfers do not guarantee placement for siblings or continued enrollment if District transfer requirements are not maintained.

    If you have any questions, please contact the Student Success Services Department at (951) 672-1851.
